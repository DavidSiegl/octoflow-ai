- **Question**: the research question in one sentence, and why the answer matters to the human.
- **Hypotheses**: what the human expects, and the result that would refute each.
- **Data and sources**: what may be used (datasets, benchmarks, literature), where it comes from, and what may not.

- **Publishable**: what an answer must show for the human to accept it, including a negative one.
