## No turn lasts less than 0s

- **A turn whose agent answers at once no longer shows as `-1s`.** A turn's start is the host's `date`, its end the
  handoff file's mtime, and Linux stamps an mtime from a clock that trails `date`'s by a few milliseconds, so a file
  written that soon after a start at the turn of a second was stamped in the second before. Such an end now counts
  as the turn's start: summary.md, `show`, the pull request's timings and metrics.json give the turn `0s`, and its
  `ended_at` is never before its `started_at`.
