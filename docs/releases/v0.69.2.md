## Both quality gates judge a round behind one interface

- **Quality rounds run as before, on Jenkins and on the local gate.** A run saved mid-quality by 0.69.1 resumes into
  the same step of its Jenkins build (trigger, queue, build, reports); `state.json` is unchanged.
- **A quality gate's checks before a run come in one order** for `run`, `resume` turning the gate on, and `doctor`'s
  local-gate check: gitleaks, then origin (Jenkins only), then the gate's own.
- **A gate that fails to stop says "the quality gate"** in its log line, for Jenkins and the local gate alike (it said
  "the local gate").
