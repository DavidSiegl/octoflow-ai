## The app's agent views stay where you scrolled

An agent's conversation (a run over ACP) and its herdr pane follow their newest output only while you are at their
end; scroll back to the end and they follow again. Scroll up in the conversation and it stays put as new output
arrives, until its agent starts a new session, which opens at its end. The conversation also no longer scrolls the
whole window down to itself each time its agent says something.

A herdr pane shows only its agent's last 200 lines, so once its agent has printed more, it no longer jumps to its end
but its text still moves up under you as each new line pushes the oldest out.
