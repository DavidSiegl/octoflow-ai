## A long commit and pull request title is cut at a word, with "…"

A run's commit and pull request take their title from the spec's title, or without one from the first line of the
task. A title longer than 72 characters was cut after its 72nd, mid-word as well, with no sign that anything was cut;
it is now cut at its last word boundary that fits and ends with "…".

A task written with Windows line endings no longer leaves a carriage return at the end of the title taken from its
first line.
