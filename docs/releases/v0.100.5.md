## A long commit and pull request title is cut at a word, with "…"

A run takes its commit's and pull request's title from its spec's title, or without one from the first line of its
task, and cut a title longer than 72 characters wherever they ended, mid-word as well, with no sign that anything was
cut. Such a title is now cut at its last word boundary that fits and ends with "…".

A task written with Windows line endings no longer leaves a carriage return at the end of the title taken from its
first line.
