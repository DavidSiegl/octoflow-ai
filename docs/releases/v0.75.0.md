## An About dialog in the desktop app

- **A bigger logo.** The top bar's logo is 64px and the name beside it larger; on a narrow window the project bar
  still wraps below them.
- **About.** Clicking the logo or the name, or pressing Enter on it, opens About: the release version, as
  `orchestrator --version` prints it, "© 2026 David Siegl", the license (MIT OR Apache-2.0), and links to the
  repository, its releases, Ko-fi and the two third-party license files the app bundles. Escape or Close dismisses it.
- **Links open outside the app**, in the system's browser or HTML viewer, through Tauri's opener plugin. The app may
  open `https` links and the two bundled license files, nothing else. A development build bundles no license files
  and says so in their place.
