## A resumed interview waits for you without telling you again

- **`resume` no longer repeats that the Spec Collector is waiting for you.** A run resumed while its interview was
  under way, the Spec Collector still running and already asked for the spec, focused its pane and notified
  "Spec Collector is waiting for you" again, once per resume. Now `resume` only waits for it: you were told when the
  interview began. A Spec Collector that `resume` starts, restarts or resumes is still announced, as is a blocked
  turn's wait for your answer.
