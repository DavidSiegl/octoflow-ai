## A turn's time says how much of it was spent waiting for your answer

In a run the desktop app drives, an agent that asks a permission waits until you answer it, and that wait was counted
as the agent's time: a review that took the agent under 30 seconds could show as 3m 44s. Each turn now records how
many of its seconds the agent spent waiting for your answers, as `waited_seconds` in `state.json` and `metrics.json`,
and `summary.md` and the pull request's "Run timings" show it beside the duration: "3m 44s (3m 19s waiting)". A run in
herdr records no such waits and is shown as before.
